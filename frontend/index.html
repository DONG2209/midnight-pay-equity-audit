Midnight · Level 4 · Confidential Pay-Equity Audit

Prove your pay gap is within the law — without revealing a single salary

Under the EU Pay Transparency Directive, an unjustified gender pay gap of 5% or more in any category of workers triggers a joint pay assessment. This dApp proves — in zero knowledge, with the real compiled pay-equity.compact contract running client-side — that every category is within the limit, disclosing only a single yes/no and a commitment to the payroll. See exactly what an observer can and cannot learn.

1 · Connect (as the employer)

Connect a Midnight wallet (e.g. Lace) through the DApp Connector API. The wallet signs one message; that signature derives your employer audit key and payroll salt, so only you can author an audit for this contract. No salary is ever involved in signing.

      Privacy model

      An observer CAN learn

      • The compliance threshold this contract enforces (e.g. 5%)
      • The single yes/no verdict of the latest audit
      • The reporting-period label and how many categories were covered
      • A hiding commitment to the payroll, and a one-way hash of the employer's identity
      • How many audits have been run (the round counter)

      An observer CANNOT learn

      • Any individual salary, or any category's average or total pay
      • The actual size of the gap — only whether it is within the limit
      • Which category (if any) came closest to failing
      • The head counts, so small categories can't be reverse-engineered
      • The employer's secret key — only a one-way hash of it is ever public

      What runs where: your wallet connects and signs to derive your key, and the circuits execute in this browser tab against an in-memory ledger — no transaction is submitted to a network in this sandbox. Submitting these same circuit calls to Midnight Preprod additionally requires a deployed contract address plus indexer and proof-server providers wired through @midnight-ntwrk/midnight-js-contracts — see scripts/deploy-preprod.ts and the README for the exact steps.
